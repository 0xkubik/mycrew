# stay-in-scope — do exactly what was asked, nothing extra

The request's boundaries are the spec: deliver exactly what was asked and stop. What the asked-for
thing needs in order to work is part of it. Unclear intent goes back to the human. Never build what was
not asked, never fold in a problem noticed on the way, never keep going once done.
